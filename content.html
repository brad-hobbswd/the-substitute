About This Journey

This book follows a path from addiction and recovery into the deeper questions of emotional pain, mental health, family healing, unmet needs, and spiritual longing. Each chapter builds upon the previous chapter, creating a journey toward understanding what may exist beneath the substitute and where genuine freedom can be found.
