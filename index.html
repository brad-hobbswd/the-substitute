Looking Beyond Addiction to Discover What Is Really Missing

What if addiction is not the deepest problem? What if substances, compulsions, and destructive behaviors are often substitutes for something deeper that has gone unnoticed for years? Drawing from personal experience as a family member affected by addiction and lessons learned through Al-Anon, The Substitute explores addiction through the lenses of recovery, emotional wounds, family pain, healing, and faith while asking one central question: What is the addiction substituting for?

About This Book

What if addiction is not the deepest problem?

For generations, addiction has often been viewed primarily through the lens of substances, compulsive behaviors, and their consequences. While those realities matter, The Substitute invites readers to consider a different possibility. What if addiction is often pointing toward something deeper that existed long before the addiction itself appeared?

Drawing from personal experience as a family member affected by addiction, insights gained through Al-Anon, observations from recovery communities, and a thoughtful exploration of emotional and spiritual healing, Bradley Hobbs examines the hidden needs that often exist beneath addictive behaviors. Throughout the book, readers are challenged to look beyond the visible struggle and ask a powerful question: What is the addiction substituting for?

Through discussions of anxiety, ADHD, trauma, grief, loneliness, shame, family pain, recovery, emotional healing, and faith, The Substitute explores how many people turn toward substances and compulsive behaviors while searching for relief, peace, acceptance, belonging, purpose, or freedom from burdens that feel impossible to carry alone.

Rather than offering simplistic answers, this book provides a compassionate framework for understanding addiction, recovery, and the human search for healing. Readers will discover that the journey beyond addiction often leads to deeper questions about identity, emotional wounds, personal responsibility, relationships, hope, and the longing for something greater than ourselves.

Whether you are personally struggling with addiction, supporting a loved one, working in recovery, or simply seeking a deeper understanding of human behavior, The Substitute offers an honest and thought provoking exploration of what may lie beneath the surface of addiction and what genuine freedom can look like beyond the substitute.

About the Author

Bradley Hobbs is a writer, educator, and advocate for healing who explores the intersections of faith, emotional recovery, personal growth, and the deeper questions that shape human experience. His work is driven by a desire to help people move beyond surface level answers and discover the deeper realities that often influence behavior, relationships, and personal struggles.

Unlike many books written from the perspective of addiction itself, The Substitute emerges from Bradley's experience as a family member affected by addiction. Through years of personal reflection, recovery education, Al-Anon participation, and a commitment to understanding the complexities of addiction and healing, he developed a growing conviction that many addictive behaviors are connected to deeper unmet needs, emotional wounds, and spiritual longings.

His writing combines thoughtful observation, lived experience, recovery principles, and Christian faith to create resources that encourage honesty, compassion, understanding, and hope. Rather than focusing solely on behaviors, Bradley seeks to help readers explore the deeper stories that exist beneath the struggles people face.

Bradley is also the author of books and devotional works that examine fear, healing, faith, emotional growth, and personal transformation. Through his writing, he encourages readers to ask meaningful questions, pursue genuine healing, and discover lasting hope through a deeper understanding of themselves and the God revealed in Jesus.

His mission is simple: to help people look beyond the visible struggle and discover the healing, freedom, and purpose that may be waiting beneath the surface.
