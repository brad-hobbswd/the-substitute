The Substitute

Looking Beyond Addiction to Discover What Is Really Missing

What if addiction is not the deepest problem? What if substances, compulsions, and destructive behaviors are often substitutes for something deeper that has gone unnoticed for years? Drawing from personal experience as a family member affected by addiction and lessons learned through Al-Anon, The Substitute explores addiction through the lenses of recovery, emotional wounds, family pain, healing, and faith while asking one central question: What is the addiction substituting for?

Read the Book

About This Book

The Substitute challenges traditional assumptions about addiction by exploring the possibility that many addictive behaviors are attempts to address deeper needs. Through discussions of anxiety, ADHD, trauma, emotional pain, recovery, family healing, and spiritual longing, readers are invited to look beyond the behavior and discover the deeper story beneath the struggle.

About the Author

Bradley Hobbs writes about healing, faith, emotional recovery, and the deeper questions that shape the human experience. Through personal reflection and lived experience, he seeks to help readers discover hope, understanding, and freedom beyond the struggles that often define their lives.
