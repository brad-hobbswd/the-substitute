The Substitute

Looking Beyond Addiction to Discover What Is Really Missing

What if addiction is not the deepest problem? What if substances, compulsions, and destructive behaviors are often substitutes for something deeper that has gone unnoticed for years? The Substitute explores addiction through the lenses of recovery, family pain, emotional wounds, healing, and faith while asking one central question: What is the addiction substituting for?

Begin Reading

About This Book

Drawing from personal experience as a family member affected by addiction and lessons learned through Al-Anon, this book examines the deeper needs that often exist beneath addictive behaviors. Rather than focusing only on substances, the journey explores anxiety, trauma, family healing, emotional pain, recovery, and the search for lasting peace.

About the Author

Bradley Hobbs writes about faith, healing, emotional recovery, and the deeper questions that shape the human experience. Through personal reflection and lived experience, he seeks to help readers discover hope, understanding, and freedom beyond the struggles that often define their lives.
