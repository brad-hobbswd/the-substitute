# The Substitute

A book exploring addiction, recovery, family pain, emotional wounds, and the deeper human search for peace, belonging, purpose, and healing.

## Live Site

https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/the-substitute

## About the Book

*The Substitute* examines addiction through a different lens.

Rather than focusing only on substances and behaviors, the book asks a deeper question:

**What is the addiction substituting for?**

Through personal experience as a family member affected by addiction, observations from Al-Anon, and exploration of emotional, psychological, and spiritual dimensions of recovery, this book investigates the needs that often exist beneath addictive behaviors.

Topics include:

* Addiction as a substitute
* Anxiety, ADHD, trauma, and misunderstood struggles
* Family member recovery and healing
* Lessons learned through Al-Anon
* The many faces of addiction
* The vacancy beneath the behavior
* Healing needs instead of fighting symptoms
* Jesus and the deepest hunger of the human heart
